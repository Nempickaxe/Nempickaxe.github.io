Strategy, Metrics, and Exam Practice

What to Read

This section covers the leadership lens: vision, use-case prioritization, measurement, responsible adoption, and exam practice links.

Exam shortcut: leadership questions usually reward strategy, measurable outcomes, ethics, compliance, and change management over tool-first answers.

Six-step GenAI Strategy

  1. Establish a clear vision.
  2. Prioritize high-impact use cases.
  3. Invest in capabilities: platforms, people, reusable patterns, and governance.
  4. Drive organizational change and adoption.
  5. Measure and demonstrate business value.
  6. Champion responsible AI: fairness, transparency, privacy, security, and accountability.
